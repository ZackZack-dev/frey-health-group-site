What you get

The things that make a difference day to day.

Flexibility in how you deliver care

In-clinic, mobile or telehealth, or a mix that suits the client and you. Most of our clinicians blend at least two delivery modes. We design the week around clinical fit and what the client needs.

Senior clinical oversight on every case

Whether you're a new graduate or a 20-year clinician, you'll have a senior to talk to. Complex cases get case-conferenced. Risk and safeguarding processes are documented. No one carries a hard case alone.

Structured supervision and CPD

Supervision is scheduled into the week and protected. We support AHPRA-aligned supervision for provisional psychologists, supervision hours for early-career OTs and speech pathologists, and a CPD allowance that recognises meaningful training, from courses to supervision beyond the included hours.

Multidisciplinary case-conferencing

Weekly opportunities to bring a case to OT, psychology, speech, counselling and behaviour support peers. You'll learn faster, write better reports, and make better referrals out when something isn't your scope.

NDIS-fluent admin behind you

Service agreements, plan-manager chasing, invoicing and reschedules are handled by people who genuinely understand the NDIS, Aged Care Support at Home and DVA. You shouldn't lose three hours a week to admin you didn't train for.

Protected time for good clinical work

Caseloads are set so you can write the report, prepare for the session, and ring the parent back. Face-to-face expectations stay reasonable, with non-billable clinical time protected.

Allied Health Assistant support where it fits

Our AHAs run program-based sessions under your delegation. That keeps therapy consistent between your sessions and stretches NDIS budgets for your clients without diluting your clinical oversight.

A path to grow with the practice

We're building a multidisciplinary practice across the country over the next few years. Senior roles, supervisor roles, state and regional leadership and program development opportunities will keep opening up. If you want to grow with us, we want to talk.

How hiring works

What happens from first chat to first week.

We respect your time. It's usually four to five short steps, scheduled around your current role. We won't ghost you or drag it out.

01

First chat

A 20 to 30 minute call with our Clinical Operations Coordinator or Director. We talk about your work, what you're after, and whether the role fits. No formal interview structure, no surprise scenarios.

02

Qualifications and registration check

We confirm AHPRA registration, professional body membership, Working with Children Check, NDIS Worker Screening and references. We move fast on this once you give us the go-ahead.

03

Meet the team

Sit down (in person or via video) with two or three clinicians you'd be working alongside, including a senior clinician in your discipline. We talk about clinical approach, a case or two, and how you'd fit into the team.

04

Shadow or case discussion

Depending on the role, you'll either shadow a session or two, or work through a current case with one of our senior clinicians. This is also where you get to ask the harder questions about caseloads, oversight and culture.

05

Offer and onboarding

Written offer, agreed start date, and a structured onboarding plan. You'll meet your supervisor, set up systems access, walk through file standards and start with a paced caseload. First six weeks include weekly check-ins with your supervisor and the operations team.

Career FAQs

Questions clinicians ask us.

Where are you hiring? +

Across Australia. We're hiring in every capital city, and Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane are where we're growing fastest. We're also hiring in Gympie and the Wide Bay, where the group started. Grove Support Services works Australia-wide, and a lot of our allied health work runs mobile or via telehealth. If you're outside a capital city, still get in touch.

Do you offer supervision for provisional psychologists? +

Yes. We have AHPRA-approved supervisors in the team and we structure supervision around the internship or registrar program requirements. Provisional psychologists carry a paced caseload and have weekly supervision plus monthly group supervision.

Can I work part-time, remote, or via telehealth? +

Yes to all three. We have clinicians working part-time across most disciplines, and we deliver via clinic, mobile and telehealth. If telehealth or mobile suits the client and you, we'll work out the model together.

What kind of caseload mix should I expect? +

It depends on the role and discipline, but most of our clinicians see a mix of NDIS, aged care, DVA and private clients across the lifespan. You can lean towards an age group or population if that's where your strengths are. We protect non-billable time for report writing, case-conferencing and supervision.

How does supervision and clinical oversight work for new graduates? +

New grads get a paced caseload, weekly individual supervision with a senior in your discipline, fortnightly multidisciplinary case-conferencing, and a structured first-six-months development plan. You won't be handed a complex NDIS Functional Capacity Assessment in your first month and left to figure it out.

Do I need to be AHPRA-registered? +

If you're in a regulated profession (psychology, occupational therapy, speech pathology, physiotherapy, social work for behaviour support) then yes, AHPRA registration (or the equivalent regulator, for example SPA for speech pathology) is non-negotiable. Counsellors need a Diploma of Counselling minimum and PACFA or ACA membership (or eligibility). Provisional psychologists need provisional AHPRA registration and an approved supervision pathway.

What about CPD, training and conference allowances? +

We have a CPD budget per clinician each year that covers approved training, supervision over and above the included hours, and one conference or major workshop. We also run internal training across the team. Recent topics include trauma-informed assessment, AAC for non-speaking clients and NDIS report writing.

Is there any reason I wouldn't be a fit here? +

It comes down to fit. Our team leans on case-conferencing, peer review and supervision, so clinicians who prefer to work alone tend to be happier elsewhere. Our practice is neuro-affirming, and we look for people who already work this way. Caseloads here are paced for quality over volume. We would rather be clear about all of that early than waste your time.

I don't see my role listed. Should I still get in touch? +

Yes. We're growing across psychology, OT, speech, counselling, play therapy, AHA, behaviour support and clinical operations. If your discipline or location isn't listed today, send your CV and a short note about what you're looking for. We keep an active pipeline and we'd rather hear from you early than miss the right person.
