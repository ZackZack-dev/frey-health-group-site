Who we are

A health group built around people, not paperwork.

Frey Health Group brings medical care, allied health and disability support together in one place. Start with any service, and your care moves with you, so nothing falls through the gaps between providers.

Warm enough for your family to feel at home, and joined up enough that your therapists and support workers work from the same page. One group. One story to tell, once.

How we work

Values that hold the whole grove together.

Person-centred

Care shaped around the person, their goals and the people around them.

Locally rooted

Born in Queensland and built on relationships. We are part of the community we serve.

Clinically credible

Warm does not mean casual. Our standards hold up for the referrers and clinicians who work with us.

One connected team

Three brands that talk to each other, so you never fall through the cracks between them.

We believe people grow best together.
The kind of care you would want for your own family
belongs to everyone, wherever they call home.

Growth is never forced.
It is nurtured through connection,
encouragement and genuine care.

Whether someone is building independence, getting support at home,
or working with a therapist, they are held
by a team that helps them thrive, season after season.

Our roots are in Queensland. The grove now reaches families right across Australia, and no one in it stands alone. Every tree shelters the next, and the whole grove grows stronger for it.
