Frey Health Group Graduate Program

Step into practice, supported every step.

A paid, early-career program for allied health graduates, run through Constellation Therapies. It comes with $20,000 of internal CPD, structured supervision, mentoring and a multidisciplinary team. You gain clinical experience from your first weeks, never left to figure it out alone.

Express your interest Explore the program

Why we built this

Graduating is exciting, and your first role is where it all takes shape. Our program makes sure graduates are supported, not left to figure it all out alone.

The program is paid. Each graduate also receives $20,000 of internal CPD to invest in their learning. Through shadowing, supervision, mentoring and a gradual move into day-to-day practice, you build confidence and capability across community, clinic and telehealth settings. You learn to adapt your therapy for children, adults, families and their support networks.

Who it's for

Graduates across allied health

Designed for early-career clinicians across a growing range of disciplines, working with children, adults, families and their support networks.

Occupational Therapy Speech Pathology Psychology Social Work Physiotherapy Dietetics Behaviour Support and more as we grow

What the program may include

A supported path into practice

+ Shadowing experienced clinicians
+ Gradual transition into seeing your own clients
+ Community-based practice in homes, schools and natural environments
+ Clinic-based therapy
+ Telehealth experience where appropriate
+ Clinical supervision and mentoring
+ Multidisciplinary learning opportunities
+ Professional development workshops
+ Peer learning and case discussions
+ Support with documentation, reports and clinical reasoning

The structure may continue to evolve as Frey Health Group grows, staying responsive, current and evidence-informed.

In practice

Genuine clinical experience, with a mentor beside you

From your first weeks you shadow experienced clinicians and gradually take on your own caseload, never thrown in the deep end. Every session is backed by supervision, feedback and a team that wants you to do well.

+ Shadow first, then lead
+ Supervision on every case
+ Clinic, community and telehealth
+ Feedback that builds confidence

Our philosophy

Great clinicians are developed

We believe great clinicians grow through support, reflection and experience. Our approach is built around four commitments.

01

Safe Practice

Supported to work within your scope while gradually building independence and confidence.

02

Supported Learning

We value curiosity, questions, mentoring and ongoing professional development.

03

Whole-Person Care

We see clients in the context of their family, community, culture, relationships and environment.

04

Sustainable Careers

Meaningful, long-term careers where you keep learning, stay well and feel connected to your work.

Clinical supervision Peer learning Safe practice Genuine caseload Ongoing feedback Team support

Discipline-specific development

Depth in your discipline

Alongside shared learning, graduates develop discipline-specific skills depending on their role and profession.

Functional assessments, sensory processing, assistive technology, home modifications, environmental assessments and capacity-building interventions.

Language assessment, speech sound disorders, AAC, feeding foundations, literacy support and communication partner coaching.

Assessment processes, therapeutic formulation, mental health presentations, neurodevelopmental assessments, cognitive and behavioural interventions and report writing.

Functional mobility, exercise prescription, falls prevention, neurological rehabilitation and community rehabilitation.

Functional behaviour assessment, positive behaviour support, restrictive practice considerations, data collection and behaviour analysis.
