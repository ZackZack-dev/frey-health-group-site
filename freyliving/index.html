Welcome to Frey Living

Frey Living is designed for people who want the freedom of independent living, with the peace of mind that comes from knowing support is available whenever it's needed.

Each resident has their own private bedroom, ensuite bathroom, living space and kitchenette. The building includes four independent units, plus dedicated accommodation for an on-site 24/7 concierge.

Shared kitchen and living areas, plus outdoor gathering spaces, provide opportunities to connect with neighbours — when you choose to. You decide when you want privacy and when you want community.

"Independent living doesn't mean living alone. It means having your own space, your own choices and your own routines — with the opportunity to connect, belong and access support when you choose."

Our first residence opens late September. Additional residences are planned across Brisbane. If this location isn't your preference, you can still express interest and let us know your preferred region.
