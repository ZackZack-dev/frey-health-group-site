What Constellation does

Constellation Therapies brings many disciplines together, so therapy works as one connected team instead of a series of separate appointments.

Each person is the centre of their own constellation. The right practitioners move around them, coordinated and aligned to the goals that matter most.

Where Constellation focuses

Therapy that works together

  • 01

    Many disciplines, one team

    Occupational therapy, speech pathology, psychology, counselling, positive behaviour support, play therapy, physiotherapy and dietetics, coordinated around one plan.

  • 02

    Goal-led therapy

    Therapy shaped by the outcomes that matter to each person.

  • 03

    Every age and stage

    Support for children, teens, adults and families, matched to where each person is.

  • 04

    Neuro-affirming, connected care

    Registered and certified clinicians who talk to each other, so progress in one area supports the rest.
